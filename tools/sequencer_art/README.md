# Sequencer art (Oct 16)
Tyrone's approved DRUM SEQUENCER picture (mock.png, 1536x1024) is the plate of the sequencer screen,
like the faceplates: `SeqPlate` is the picture with the live areas cleared (tool labels, transport icons,
chip texts, the rack, the grid, the left panel). Sprites cut from it: `SeqCell` (one step button), `SeqRow`
(a rack row with the name blanked), `SeqKnob`, the left panel's section headers and the GENERATE box.
The rectangles the controls sit on are in `SequencerView.A` (picture pixels). Rebuild with build_art.py.
