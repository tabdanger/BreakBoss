## How to build and ship
- Source of truth: `BreakBoss.swiftpm/` (Swift Playgrounds package). `xcode/generate.py` makes the Xcode
  project (app + AUv3 plug-in) on CI. Plug-in skips `BreakBossApp.swift`, `AppAudioHost.swift`, `KnockSelfTest.swift`.
- `.github/workflows/build.yml`: build + self-test (`KnockSelfTest.swift`) on simulators. Push commits with
  `[skip ci]` in the message to skip it (Tyrone asked to skip the test build to save Actions minutes).
- `.github/workflows/upload.yml`, `mode`:
  - `upload`: archive, sign, upload, give the build to internal testers (~15-20 min, macOS).
  - `external`: test notes + external group "BreakBoss Beta" + Beta App Review (Linux, cheap). Review contact is
    copied from Tyrone's other app (Console EQE). Only one build can be in beta review at a time.
  - Public TestFlight link: https://testflight.apple.com/join/2AzCejab
  - Dispatch: `curl -X POST -H "Accept: application/vnd.github+json" https://api.github.com/repos/tabdanger/BreakBoss/actions/workflows/upload.yml/dispatches -d '{"ref":"main","inputs":{"mode":"upload"}}'`
- Tester notes: `.github/testflight/what-to-test.txt`, `beta-description.txt`.

## MIDI (the plug-in is a MIDI instrument)
- MIDI OUT (`KnockEngine.midiOutHit`, AU `midiOutputNames`, `midiOutputEventBlock` kept current via override):
  multi-out by default: pad n on MIDI channel n+1, drum note 36+n; the 808 on channel 7, chromatic around C3
  (60 = root, 48...84). "One channel" layout: drums on channel 10 (GM notes), the 808 stays on channel 7.
- MIDI IN round trip (`KnockEngine.outRoute`): notes shaped like MIDI OUT always play the drum itself
  (ONE-SHOT and LOOP mode), the 808 ends on note-off (recorded length), drums ring out. Notes from the DAW
  are never echoed back out. Other notes keep the normal pad map / LOOP launching (controllers).
- Logic Pro for iPad: record BreakBoss MIDI by making a Software Instrument track whose MIDI input is
  BreakBoss (Internal MIDI In), arm, record. To have the recorded part play BreakBoss: record onto BreakBoss's
  own track (its MIDI input set to BreakBoss; the 50 ms loop-back guard stops double hits) and press BreakBoss
  STOP so only the DAW's part plays.
- FOLLOW DAW: BreakBoss STOP mutes the pattern while the DAW runs; PLAY brings it back on the DAW's beat;
  with the DAW stopped PLAY runs BreakBoss at the DAW's tempo (`userArmed` / `localRun` in KnockEngine).

## Drum roll (DrumRollView.swift)
- Pinch / trackpad spread zooms: normal -> 1 bar -> 1/2 bar -> 1 beat; SNAP follows (1/16, 1/32, 1/64, 1/128).
- Pattern length 2 / 4 / 8 / 16 bars.
- SELECT: drag the right edge of selected notes to trim (longer/shorter); drum note lengths gate the sound and
  the MIDI note length.
- Channel row: IMPORT button (was play) opens `SoundLibraryView` (all sounds by type and pack, import single
  sounds or whole pack folders into Documents/Samples).
- SNAP TO SCALE button replaced PIANO ROLL (key, FL scales, fit notes).
- Dice: lane dice = velocities only; channel dice = that channel's notes; top dice = sounds + pattern;
  faceplate dice = sounds only.
- RECORD: drum roll record button, and on the faceplate hold PLAY (0.5 s) to arm MPC-style recording (red ring);
  finger-played pads land in the playing pattern quantized to 1/16 (`KnockController.recordSnap`).
