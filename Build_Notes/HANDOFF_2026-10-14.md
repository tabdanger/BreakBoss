## Audio engine (Oct 17)
- LEVEL / PAN are applied while rendering (`KitBuffers.mixL/mixR`, `Voice.mixL/mixR`), gliding 10 ms, so a
  ringing note follows the knob. A kit handoff only fades notes whose pad's sample changed (`===`); knob turns
  used to fade every note on every tick, which was the clicking.
- TEXTURE has WARP (0 = off) and WOBBLE (rate: 1 bar ... 1/32) in Menu > Engine controls (`ModeProcessor.swift`):
  two-head pitch shifter swinging up to +/-12 st, resonant SVF sweeping against it, and stutters that repeat the
  first 1/32-1/64 of some 1/16s (sometimes reversed), synced to the transport beat while playing.

## MIDI (the plug-in is a MIDI instrument)
- MIDI OUT (`KnockEngine.midiOutHit`, AU `midiOutputNames`, `midiOutputEventBlock` kept current via override):
  multi-out by default: pad n on MIDI channel n+1, drum note 36+n; the 808 on channel 7, chromatic around C3
  (60 = root, 48...84). "One channel" layout: drums on channel 10 (GM notes), the 808 stays on channel 7.
- Oct 16: a drum on a MIDI channel of its own (`ownerOfChannel`) sends its pitch as the note (its note +
  semis), and ANY note on that channel coming back plays it at that pitch (`.padPitched` event) and records
  it. So a DAW piano roll has full control of each pad's pitch and velocity. Shared channels keep exact notes.
- MIDI IN round trip (`KnockEngine.outRoute`): notes shaped like MIDI OUT always play the drum itself
  (ONE-SHOT and LOOP mode), the 808 ends on note-off (recorded length), drums ring out. Notes from the DAW
  are never echoed back out. Other notes keep the normal pad map / LOOP launching (controllers).
- MIDI files: `MIDIFile.writeChannels` (the sequencer's cloud button) = type-1 file, a track per pad on
  channel n+1, notes around C3, channel names as track names. `MIDIFile.read` takes those (notes on channels
  other than 10 → channel = pad, pitch = note − 60) and the old GM channel-10 files.

## RECORD (Oct 16)
- `KEvent.countIn` (a = beats, value = loop): the transport starts below zero, the metronome clicks until the
  pattern's first beat, nothing plays or records before it; `status[8]` = beats left. Clicks also run while
  record is armed. `KnockController.toggleRecordPads(loop:)`, `countInBars` (1 / 2, menu), `countingIn`.
- Face 1: `RecordButtonView` in the top-right row (left of the piano-roll button, rect derived from
  `editorRect`). Face 2: the REC transport button and Drum Track. Hold PLAY still arms too.
- Logic Pro for iPad: record BreakBoss MIDI by making a Software Instrument track whose MIDI input is
  BreakBoss (Internal MIDI In), arm, record. To have the recorded part play BreakBoss: record onto BreakBoss's
  own track (its MIDI input set to BreakBoss; the 50 ms loop-back guard stops double hits) and press BreakBoss
  STOP so only the DAW's part plays.
- FOLLOW DAW: BreakBoss STOP mutes the pattern while the DAW runs; PLAY brings it back on the DAW's beat;
  with the DAW stopped PLAY runs BreakBoss at the DAW's tempo (`userArmed` / `localRun` in KnockEngine).

## Sequencer (SequencerView.swift, Oct 16) — replaced the drum roll
- Tyrone's picture is the plate (`tools/sequencer_art/`): `build_controls.py` cuts every button into a sprite
  and paints it out of the plate; `build_finishes.py` makes the black / cream / blue plates and rack rows so
  Face 2 wears Face 1's finish (`controller.faceKey`). Controls are `SeqButton` / `SeqChip` / `SeqGlyph`
  (SeqControls.swift) with the `SeqPress` pressed look; menus are BBMenu (attach `.bbMenu` BEFORE `.place`,
  or use `bbMenuPlaced`, or the touch area becomes the whole screen).
- Rack = `controller.rackChannels` (12 max, each a pad); drag a library sound onto it. The arrow opens a
  track's piano roll with a KEY / SCALE / SNAP / FIT toolbar; the lane under the grid edits the open track.
  A fresh project (INIT) opens with a blank grid. Dice per track, SWING / HUMANIZE sliders, BPM to 280.

## Drum roll (DrumRollView.swift) — gone; kept here for the ideas that moved into the sequencer
- Pinch / trackpad spread zooms: normal -> 1 bar -> 1/2 bar -> 1 beat; SNAP follows (1/16, 1/32, 1/64, 1/128).
- Pattern length 2 / 4 / 8 / 16 bars.
- SELECT: drag the right edge of selected notes to trim (longer/shorter); drum note lengths gate the sound and
  the MIDI note length.
- Channel row: IMPORT button (was play) opens `SoundLibraryView` (all sounds by type and pack, import single
  sounds or whole pack folders into Documents/Samples).
- SNAP TO SCALE button replaced PIANO ROLL (key, FL scales, fit notes).
- Dice: lane dice = velocities only; channel dice = that channel's notes; top dice = sounds + pattern;
  faceplate dice = sounds only.
- RECORD: drum roll record button, and on the faceplate hold PLAY (0.5 s) to arm MPC-style recording (red ring);
  finger-played pads land in the playing pattern quantized to 1/16 (`KnockController.recordSnap`).
